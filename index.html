Our Policies

School Policies

Your safety and learning experience always come first.

General School Policies

Dress Code

Students must be dressed appropriately for every driving lesson.

Lesson Expiry

Lesson packages expire 18 months after purchase.

Lesson Duration

Each lesson includes 55 minutes of driving and a 5-minute review.

Scheduling

Lessons are scheduled through our office via phone, text, or email.

Driver's License

Bring your valid driver's license to every lesson.

Vehicle Occupancy

Maximum of three people (including one observer) per lesson.

Road Test

Bring two government-issued IDs and your driver's license.

Outstanding Fees

All outstanding fees must be paid before your scheduled road test.

ICBC Fees

Road test and licensing fees are paid directly to ICBC.

Refund Policy

There are no refunds for lessons that have already been taken.

Cancellation Policy

Cancel at least 36 hours before your scheduled lesson.
Same-day cancellations are charged the full lesson fee.
Road tests require 72 hours notice for cancellation or rescheduling.
ICBC cancellation fees remain the student's responsibility.
